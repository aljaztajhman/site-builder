# Owner's phone run (M2 criterion 5)

About 15 minutes, on your own phone, on mobile data or Wi-Fi. Do it after Claude says the live app has the M2 changes and sends email from mail.postavi.si. One full run costs about €0.30–0.80 on the live app's daily cap.

Write down anything odd as you go (a screenshot is enough): slow, confusing, wrong Slovene, something cut off, a button too small.

## Before you start
- [ ] Have 3–6 photos of a business on the phone (yours, a friend's, or any real one you know).
- [x] Your email is on the allow-list (plan Osnovni, done 2026-10-18), so you can make the whole site and publish.

## The run
1. [ ] Open https://app.postavi.si on the phone (the app moved there on 2026-10-19; sign-in links point there) (not signed in; use a private tab if you were signed in before). The first screen makes sense without zooming.
2. [ ] Without signing in, fill in the business: name, a few sentences about it, phone, town. Add your photos from the gallery (or take one with the camera). Every photo is accepted. If a photo is refused, note which phone and what it said (iPhone photos are HEIC). If a "Preverjamo, da niste robot" check appears, it passes.
3. [ ] Tap "Ustvari stran". The first preview appears within a minute. Note the seconds. The preview has the small "Predogled" badge, and publishing asks you to sign in.
4. [ ] Sign in with your email to keep the preview. The sign-in email arrives within a minute, from `prijava@mail.postavi.si`, and is not in spam. Tap the link and confirm. Your preview is now in your account.
5. [ ] Look at the page on the phone: your photos are used, nothing is invented (no fake reviews, prices, hours or names you didn't give), the text reads like Slovene.
6. [ ] Make the whole site (all pages), if it asks.
7. [ ] Change something by chat, e.g. "Dodaj delovni čas: pon–pet 8–16" or "Naj bo glava temnejša". The change shows and the reply makes sense.
8. [ ] Change something by tapping the preview: one heading, one photo (swap it for another of yours).
9. [ ] Fill in anything it asks for under "Še to potrebujemo".
10. [ ] Tap "Objavi". Open the published page in the phone's browser. It looks the same as the preview.
11. [ ] On the published page, send a message through the contact form (if there is one). The email "Novo sporočilo s spletne strani …" reaches your inbox; replying goes to the address you typed in the form.
12. [ ] Open the same site on a computer once: it looks good at full width too.

## When you're done
Tell Claude in chat: "phone run done", with your notes. Claude turns each note into a fix or a TASKS item. Criterion 5 is ticked when the run went through without help.
