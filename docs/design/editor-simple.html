Simple editor: point at it, or say it

The owner's note: "make it as simple as possible and easy to understand; it feels like a lot is going on". The editor can already do a lot. The problem is that it shows all of it at once.

Short answer

  1. The site is the editor. Tap a text to type, tap a picture to swap it, tap a section to get its toolbar right on the page. The six tabs go.
  2. One box for everything else. "Kaj naj spremenimo?" sits under the preview on every screen: "dodaj delovni čas", "toplejše barve", "nova stran za cenik". The assistant moves from a hidden tab to the centre of the screen. This is how we can be the most powerful and the easiest at once.
  3. The panel shows only what you clicked. Nothing selected: what's left before publishing, and three shortcuts (Podatki, Oblika, Strani).
  4. Nothing internal on screen. Euro spend, version numbers, pixel widths, hex codes, character counters and the "Urejanje s klikom" checkbox all leave the owner's view.
  5. Measured, not guessed. Six everyday owner tasks are scripted in the browser, with the taps counted before and after. Target: each one done in 3 taps or fewer, on a phone.

Five rules

  • Point at itAnything visible on the page is edited by tapping it on the page. The panel follows the selection.
  • Or say itAnything else is one sentence in the box under the preview. The direct edits stay free; the box costs cents and says so once, not on every screen.
  • Show what's needed nowOptional fields stay behind "Več možnosti". A field you empty becomes "missing" by itself; no separate button.
  • Nothing internalNo €, no version numbers, no px, no hex, no logs in the owner's view. They live in an admin view for us.
  • One word per thing"Razdelek", "Stran", "Podatki", "Oblika", "Objavi". The same words in the panel, the toolbar and the assistant's replies.

How we build it (each step shippable)

  1. Remove the noise. Spend pill, version number, px label, click-edit checkbox, counters under 80 %, "Označi kot manjkajoče" (emptying a field marks it), checklist banner → chip. Small, safe, immediate.
  2. The assistant box under the preview, with suggestions; the Pomočnik tab goes.
  3. Section toolbar and "+" on the page, tap-a-picture actions; the Vsebina and Fotografije tabs go.
  4. Panel by selection, plus the three shortcuts (Podatki, Oblika, Strani); the tab row goes.
  5. Oblika for owners: style cards, one colour, fonts as samples, advanced folded.
  6. Phone layout: full-screen site, one-row app bar, dock with the box.

The check for every step: a browser script runs six owner tasks at 375 and 1280 px and counts taps. The target is 3 or fewer each, and no task may get worse. The six tasks: change the phone number, change Saturday hours, replace a generated picture with one's own, add an FAQ, make the colours warmer, publish. The script also runs axe and checks for horizontal scroll. Today's counts are measured first and become the baseline.
