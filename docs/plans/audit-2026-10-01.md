## Cost levers, best first

1. **Pictures at medium quality** (config `imageGen.models.gpt-image-2.5.params.quality` + price). Saves ~€0.027 per picture at table prices (€0.053 per photo-less homepage, €0.080 per full site). Risk: the comparison picked GPT Image because high quality looked like an owner's phone photo. Verify: `pnpm fixtures:ai-photos compare` with a medium variant (~€0.09), look at the sheet, then `pnpm eval --scope home --only instalacije-rebernik --judge` and `--only racunovodstvo-seliskar --judge`.
2. **Reuse on rebuild.** "Ustvari znova", "Poskusi znova" and homepage → full site redo brief, design, alt text and every generated picture; a failed job's pictures are paid again on retry. Keeping them saves €0.07–0.18 per rebuild and keeps the full site looking like the preview the owner liked. Offer "new pictures" as an explicit choice. Verify: pipeline test that a second `generateSite` only fills missing picture slots.
3. **1-hour cache TTL on the section catalogue** (first system block, 15.9k tokens). Costs €0.020 extra per write, saves €0.031 per request in that hour that would otherwise miss; a cold edit is €0.053 vs €0.016 warm. Measure the production cache hit rate per stage first (`model_calls` cache read vs write), then decide. Also remove the brief/design/alt breakpoints if they stay unread (~€0.003 per job).
4. **Repair content in code before a paid retry** (done for SEO title length and key whitespace, see TASKS), and ask for RFC 6902 patches instead of "the complete corrected JSON" on what code can't fix: ~300 output tokens instead of 3.3–5.2k, and no resending of the previous answer as uncached input.
5. **Second critique round only when the re-check after round 1 reports failures.** 3 of 4 second rounds returned no patches (~€0.022 each). Risk: on gostilna round 2 caught a round-1 fix that didn't take. Verify: `--scope home --only frizerstvo-lana,gostilna-zlata-zlica,zobozdravstvo-lebar --judge`.
6. **Eval spend**: cache fal pictures by prompt hash in live/record runs (€0.54 of a €2.97 run); judge through the Batch API (50 % off) for baseline runs; `--no-judge` while iterating.
7. **Critique input**: send only the homepage's spec, chrome and business (it only sees homepage screenshots): ~2.4k tokens less per call.
8. **Alt text on Haiku 4.5**: ~€0.004 per site; risk on Slovene quality and `heroSuitable`. Not brief or design (they drive facts, pages and distinctiveness).

Not worth it: lowering effort, batching alt text (already one call), a shared picture library per trade (same picture on two sites in one town).

## Prompts: what to change, best first

All of these change model output: recordings go stale, and each needs the named `pnpm eval --only` runs before merging (CLAUDE.md). Line numbers as of commit 6355fd2.

**Status 2026-10-07 (branch `claude/prompt-fixes`):** items 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 10 and 11 are written, each behind its own switch in config `promptFixes` (all off; docs/dev/prompt-fixes.md), plus item 8 as recommended here: the brief no longer carries the full RULES (switch `brief`, with an address rule for the fizioterapija-pregib miss). Replay-tested only (no model calls): with the switches off every prompt is byte-identical to main (sha256 test); with all on, the recorded critique patches and scripted edits still apply, the same counts as with them off (docs/dev/prompt-fixes.md). Not measured yet: each needs its paid `--only` run with `--prompt-fixes <name>`. Not done: item 4 (Slovene style block) and item 9 (content message), which were not in this batch.

1. **Same-looking homepages come from the catalogue text.**
   - `packages/spec/src/sections/heroes.ts:6` eyebrow: "Short plain label above the headline, e.g. the town or trade." All 10 recorded heroes follow it ("Avtoservis v Kranju"). Proposed: "Optional; usually leave it out. Use it only for one short fact from the brief the headline doesn't carry (e.g. "Brez napotnice", "Odprto tudi ob sobotah"). Never just the trade or town: the header already names the business."
   - `structure.ts:44` contact-strip "right after the hero on local-business homepages" (10/10 put it second) and `content.ts:106` cta "Use near the end of a page" (9/10 end on a call band). Proposed cta: "Use only for an action the visitor hasn't been offered yet (booking, ordering, menu, rooms). Not to repeat call/directions: the phone bar and footer already do."
   - `packages/engine/src/prompts.ts:17` "Mobile first: most visitors are on a phone" contradicts the responsive decision and says nothing about the call button count. Proposed: one primary action per screen; call and directions are already one tap away (header on desktop, fixed bar on phones), so the hero's primary action is the most useful non-call step, except for trades where phoning is the only way to buy.
   - `prompts.ts:12` placeholders "where the schema allows it" produced "[Vnesite ceno]" in product cards: prefer leaving optional fields out.
   - Verify: `--only avtoservis-mrak --judge`, `--only frizerstvo-lana --judge`, `--only instalacije-rebernik --judge`; watch skeleton similarity, calls on the first screen, clutter and distinctiveness.
2. **Generated pictures read as stock** (brief `imageIdeas`, `prompts.ts:66`, and `imageGen.pipeline.style`). "…or the landscape around their town" gave a plumber the Haloze vineyards; the rest are styled flat lays. The pipeline style dropped the "unstaged smartphone photo, signs of use" wording that made the fixture photos look real, and "no faces" still allows hands. Proposed rule: show the materials/tools/ingredients this client names where they are really used, with signs of use; landscape only for businesses that sell the place; first idea is the hero (wider scene, subject in the middle third), others closer details; subject gives what, where, light, camera distance and angle; one good and one bad example. Proposed style: "Unstaged photo taken with a good smartphone by a Slovenian tradesperson at work: natural available light, true-to-life slightly muted colours, ordinary surroundings with small imperfections and signs of use, nothing styled or symmetrical… No people or body parts, no text or lettering anywhere…". Verify: `--scope home --judge` on instalacije-rebernik, racunovodstvo-seliskar, fizioterapija-pregib (~€0.14 of pictures each) and look at them.
3. **Critique** (`prompts.ts:76-82`, `stages.ts:383-416`): 2 of 14 answers open with prose; it "corrected" correct Slovene (terasico → teraso); it deleted dish tags for a 14 px failure that comes from a component; its checklist asks for things it can't see or patch (body text ≥ 16 px, LCP preload); it noted "too many call buttons" with no rule to act on. Proposed: priorities (repeated action/fact on a screen; type-only hero although a hero-suitable photo exists, with the photo ids passed in; trade/town-only eyebrows; first person, one number, no dual, no English), change Slovene only for clear errors, component failures go to issues and never cause deletions, JSON only with an op example. Verify: `--only gostilna-zlata-zlica`, `--only avtoservis-mrak`.
4. **Slovene style block** shared by content, edit and critique (`prompts.ts:10-11`): no English words ("prevzem v trgovini", not "click & collect"), with vikanje "we together" is plural never dual ("skupaj poiščemo", recorded "skupaj poiščeva"), quotes „…", no em dash, write as the owner says it at the counter. Verify: `--only fizioterapija-pregib`, `--only frizerstvo-lana`, `--only gostilna-zlata-zlica`.
5. **Design and directions**: industrial and soft-studio summaries still promise black-and-white/duotone photos (`directions.ts`), and `prompts.ts:47` prints `Imagery: monochrome/duotone` although photos render in true colour. Editorial lists `page-header:plain` as a homepage hero and has no photo hero, so photo sites that pick it open type-only. The design prompt's "Avoid" list (cream, pill, gradients…) is enforced by tokens already: wasted tokens.
6. **Alt text** (`prompts.ts:71`): no business context; "placeholder with a caption" is a leftover of SVG stand-ins; `heroSuitable` has no criteria. Proposed system prompt with criteria (sharp, well lit, survives 4:3 and square crops, shows the business's place/work/products; false for logos, documents, dark or cluttered shots).
7. **Judge** (`tools/eval/src/judge.ts:35-60`): penalises required placeholders and the required call bar; reads expectations into the direction id; doesn't know which pictures are generated; scores come before notes in the schema.
8. **Brief** carries all of RULES (~600 unused tokens); fizioterapija-pregib's brief returned `address: null` although the text has "Glavni trg 9, 8000 Novo mesto".
9. **Content message**: `JSON.stringify(brief)` sends English `imageIdeas` and `missing` into the content prompt; say "seo.title ≤ 55 characters".
10. **Chat edit**: owners use the editor's Slovene section names ("Poziv k dejanju"); no label reaches the prompt. Add the label → type map to the cached edit system block. One recorded edit reply came back in English (`racunovodstvo-seliskar/007-edit.json`).
11. **Classifier**: say what confidence means (0–0.2 for non-business text, ≤ 0.5 when no type fits); ties into the uncalibrated junk threshold.

Found on the way: `businessFromBrief` (`assemble.ts`) forces an hours placeholder for every non-builder, even when the client said not to publish hours (racunovodstvo-seliskar shows "[Vnesite delovni čas]").
