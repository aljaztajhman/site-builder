# Variety switches: before and after (2026-10-08)

HQ overnight plan wk-1008-thu, item it-variety-measure. All runs: `pnpm eval --record-missing --twins --scope home --no-edits --judge`, the judge in one Message Batch. Each run keeps its own recordings (`--recordings`). Generated pictures are cached, and the switched runs reuse each fixture's baseline pictures (`--reuse-pictures`), so only the design changes between runs. The numbers and per-site tables are in `eval/variety-2026-10-08/`: `off`, `on`, `fam`, `skel` and `conc`, each as `.md` and `.json`, plus `compare-*.md` from `pnpm variety:compare`.

Paid: €3.46 baseline, €3.30 switches on, €0.50 for a first switches-on attempt (stopped and restarted with a higher run limit, see "Notes"), €0.91 one switch at a time. Total €8.17.

## Decision

**No switch goes on in config.** None of the runs meets every target in docs/plans/variety-engine.md Step 0. The strongest results: families + skeleton remove every same-trade collision (11 → 0), and concept alone lifted the judge on the three weakest twins. The fixes the runs pointed at are in PRs #149 (merged) and the `it-family-type-heroes` / `it-skeleton-phone-header-wrap` work. The choice of what to turn on is the owner's decision `sb-variety-switches` in HQ.

5. The skeleton's "phone" header wraps longer business names at 360 px (elektro-zupan, salon-tina, frizerski-salon-mia). HQ `it-skeleton-phone-header-wrap`.
