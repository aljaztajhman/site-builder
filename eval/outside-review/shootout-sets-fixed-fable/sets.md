### claude (claude-fable-5-1)

Overall **3**, variety **2**. This batch is clean, legible and consistently built, but it is one template wearing ten outfits. Every homepage uses the same left-aligned headline, short paragraph, small button and a lone right-side ornament; only the colour and a line icon change. Three pages use obviously AI-generated photos with a visible label. The dark, serif-led pages (cheese dairy, watchmaker, brewery) show the studio can create mood; the light pages (bike shop, optician, winery) feel empty and generic. To move from a template shop to a studio owners would be proud of, invest in distinct hero compositions, varied type scales, and real or bespoke imagery, and fix the mobile cut-offs and overlapping buttons.

- Sameness: Every page is the same split hero: top-left wordmark + 'Domov' link, big left-aligned headline (3–4 lines), one short paragraph, a small pill button, and a single decorative element on the right (thin line-art icon or an AI photo with an 'Ustvarjeno z UI' badge). Type is two families only: a geometric grotesk (Bergant, Ovinek, Jasna, Kodra, Rov, Takt, Vrhovnik) and a serif (Simnu, Tič, Pinterič), all at near-identical sizes. Palettes alternate white/off-white vs. near-black with one accent; the pink/magenta button recurs (Bergant, Kodra, Takt). Line icons share one stroke weight and style. Mobile pages all end in the same Klic / Navodila za pot button bar.
- Recurring issues: Right half of the hero is empty or filled with a single thin line drawing, so desktop first screens feel underpopulated (Ovinek, Jasna, Tič, Pinterič, Simnu). · Headline and body sizes are nearly identical across pages, giving one voice regardless of trade. · AI-generated stock photos with a visible 'Ustvarjeno z UI' badge undermine trust (Bergant, Kodra, Vrhovnik). · Tiny pill buttons and link-sized CTAs have weak prominence against large headlines. · Mobile screens cut off awkwardly: Pinterič shows a clipped section title, Takt phone/button overlap, Jasna has a dead blue band. · Sticky bottom bar on mobile hides content and is identical on every site. · Light grey small body text has marginal contrast on dark pages.
- Best: sirarna-pr-simnu (Dark green, warm yellow serif and the bold cheese-wheel graphic feel genuinely tied to the business and carry the strongest colour identity.); urarstvo-tic (The serif type and oversized clock face give a quiet, craft-like mood that matches a watchmaker and holds up on mobile.); pivovarna-rov (The hanging-lamp line drawing and amber accent create a believable atmosphere for an ex-mine brewery.)
- Weakest: kolesarski-servis-ovinek (The green bent pipe shape means nothing, the address block clutters the top, and the right side is mostly empty.); vinarstvo-pinteric (Headline floats in a vast empty beige field with a sketch tucked in the corner; mobile shows a clipped heading.); optika-jasna (Blue service strip and tiny glasses icon sit awkwardly, leaving large dead areas and a stray empty blue band on mobile.)
- One change: Break the single hero template: give each business its own composition, type scale and image treatment (real photography, bespoke illustration or full-bleed colour), instead of swapping accent colour and a line icon in the same left-text/right-ornament layout.
